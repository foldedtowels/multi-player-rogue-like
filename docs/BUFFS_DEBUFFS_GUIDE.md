# Buffs & Debuffs Guide

A complete reference for all status effects in Deck Masters.

---

## How Status Effects Work

Status effects are applied to characters (players, allies, and enemies) and modify their stats or behavior. Effects have:
- **Stacks**: Most effects accumulate (e.g., 3 Strength = +3 damage)
- **Decay**: Effects may lose stacks or be removed over time

---

## Decay Types Explained

| Decay Type | Behavior |
|------------|----------|
| **Permanent** | Never decays. Lasts entire fight. |
| **Per Turn** | Loses 1 stack at the start of each turn. |
| **End of Turn** | Completely removed when your turn ends. |
| **After Turn Start** | Applied at turn start, then removed. |
| **End of Enemy Turn** | Removed after enemies finish attacking. |

## Buffs (Positive Effects)

### Strength 💪
**Decay:** Permanent

Each stack adds **+1 damage** to all your attacks.

**Strategy:** Strength is incredibly powerful for scaling damage. A single Strength buff early in a fight pays dividends on every attack you make.

**Found on:** Bless (Selene), Battlecry (Fabio), Battle Fury, Champion's Will, War God's Blessing

---

### Armor 🛡️
**Decay:** Permanent

Each stack reduces incoming damage by **1**.

**Strategy:** Unlike Shield (which resets each turn), Armor persists the entire fight. Stack it early against damage-heavy bosses.

**Found on:** Iron Skin, Champion's Will, various boss abilities

---

### Rested 😌
**Decay:** After Turn Start

Grants **+1 stamina** per stack at the start of your next turn.

**Strategy:** Use Rested cards at the end of your turn to have more stamina next turn. The buff persists through the enemy turn and activates when your turn begins.

**Found on:** Take a Breather (Fabio)

---

### Invigorated ⚡
**Decay:** End of Turn

When applied, immediately grants **Damage+** equal to 2× the Invigorated stacks.

**Strategy:** A burst damage enabler. Use it before playing multiple attack cards for maximum effect.

**Found on:** Power Punch (Fabio)

---

### Damage+ ⚔️
**Decay:** End of Turn

Each stack adds **+1 damage** to attacks this turn only.

**Strategy:** Temporary attack boost for the current turn. Often granted by other effects like Invigorated.

**Found on:** Alchemist's Focus (Kevin)

---

### Ring of Fire 💍
**Decay:** End of Enemy Turn

Deals **3 damage** back to any enemy that attacks you.

**Strategy:** Excellent against enemies with multi-hit attacks. Apply it before the enemy turn, and it stays active through all their attacks.

**Found on:** Ring of Fire (Kevin)

---

### Played Twice 🔁
**Decay:** Consumed on use

Your next card is played **twice**.

**Strategy:** Save this for your highest-impact card. Doubles healing, damage, or buff applications.

**Found on:** Divine Echo (Enrique)

---

### Invincible ✨
**Decay:** End of Enemy Turn

**Prevents all damage** until enemies finish attacking.

**Strategy:** The ultimate defensive buff. Use it when you know a massive attack is coming.

**Found on:** Divine Shield (Enrique)

---

## Debuffs (Negative Effects)

### Vulnerable 💔
**Decay:** Per Turn (-1 stack/turn)

Take **50% more damage** from all sources.

**Strategy:** Apply to enemies before unleashing big attacks. Stack removal prioritizes this debuff if you're targeted.

**Applied by:** Inferno Blast, boss abilities

---

### Weakness 😵
**Decay:** Per Turn (-1 stack/turn)

Each stack reduces attack damage by **1**.

**Strategy:** Excellent for reducing incoming damage from hard-hitting enemies. Stacks well for prolonged fights.

**Applied by:** Weaken (Selene), various boss abilities

---

### Hinder 🚫
**Decay:** End of Turn

Each stack reduces attack damage by **1** for this turn only.

**Strategy:** Applied by bosses to reduce your damage output. Plan around it by using non-attack cards when hindered.

**Applied by:** Boss abilities (Brock, others)

---

### Fatigued 😴
**Decay:** After Turn Start

Reduces your stamina by **1 per stack** next turn.

**Strategy:** Some powerful cards apply Fatigued to yourself as a cost. Plan your next turn around having less stamina.

**Found on:** Power Punch (Fabio), Overexertion (Enrique)

---

### Exhausted 🥵
**Decay:** Per Turn (-1 stack/turn)

**Cannot play cards** while Exhausted.

**Strategy:** A devastating self-debuff on very powerful cards. You'll need to pass your turn(s) while Exhausted wears off.

**Found on:** Rage Slam (Fabio)

---

### Scared 😨
**Decay:** End of Turn

**Cannot play Attack cards** this turn.

**Strategy:** Applied by bosses to prevent aggression. Use this turn for defense, buffs, or healing instead.

**Applied by:** Boss abilities (Brock, others)

---

### Decay 💀
**Decay:** Permanent (cannot be removed)

Reduces healing by **5 HP per stack**.

**Strategy:** A permanent downside on some powerful healing cards. At high stacks, healing becomes ineffective. Use Decay cards sparingly or when you're confident you won't need healing.

**Found on:** Dark Pact (Fabio), Sacrificial Healing (Enrique), Blood Ritual (Enrique)

---

### Wet 💧
**Decay:** None (must be removed by cards)

A debuff used for Kevin's alchemy combos. Doesn't do anything on its own but enables other card effects.

**Strategy:** Apply Wet to enemies, then use cards that deal bonus damage to Wet targets or convert Wet stacks into healing/damage.

**Applied by:** Splash (Kevin), Water Bolt (Kevin)

**Removed by:** Purify (Kevin) - heals 5 HP per Wet stack removed

---

## Damage Over Time (DOT)

### Poison ☠️
**Decay:** Per Turn (-1 stack/turn)

Deals damage equal to stacks at turn start, then loses 1 stack. **Ignores Shield.**

**Strategy:** Poison is excellent against high-shield enemies since it bypasses their defenses. The decay means you need to keep reapplying it for sustained damage.

**Applied by:** Venom Strike, boss abilities

---

### Burn 🔥
**Decay:** None (permanent)

Deals damage equal to stacks at turn start. **Ignores Shield.**

**Strategy:** Unlike Poison, Burn doesn't decay! A few Burn applications early in a fight deal damage every turn for the rest of the fight.

**Applied by:** Inferno Blast, boss abilities (Trogdor)

## Gameplay Tips

1. **Permanent buffs scale fights** - Strength and Armor applied early pay off every turn.

2. **DOT bypasses Shield** - Against shielded enemies, Poison and Burn are your best friends.

3. **Plan around self-debuffs** - Cards with Fatigued, Exhausted, or Decay costs are powerful but require planning.

4. **Vulnerability windows** - Apply Vulnerable before your biggest attacks for 50% more damage.

5. **Defensive timing** - Ring of Fire and Invincible last through the enemy turn, so apply them before ending your turn.

6. **Burn vs Poison** - Burn is better for long fights (permanent), Poison is better for short bursts (higher initial damage).

7. **Watch your Decay stacks** - Each stack reduces healing by 5. At 4+ stacks, most healing becomes negligible.
