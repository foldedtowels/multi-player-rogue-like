# Card Guide for Dummies

This is a simple guide to all cards for Fabio and Kevin.

# FABIO - The Warrior

**Health:** 50 | **Stamina per turn:** 2

**Passive Ability:** Warrior's Choice - Once per turn, pick ONE:
- Deal 2 damage to enemy
- Draw 1 card
- Give 3 shield to yourself or ally

---

# Quick Reference: What Do Buffs/Debuffs Do?

## BUFFS (Good stuff)
| Name | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Strength** | +1 damage per stack (permanent) |
| **Armor** | -1 damage taken per stack (permanent) |
| **Rested** | +1 stamina next turn, then goes away |
| **Invigorated** | +2 damage this turn |
| **Damage+** | +1 damage per stack this turn |
| **Ring of Fire** | Enemies take 3 damage when they hit you |

## DEBUFFS (Bad stuff)
| Name | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Poison** | Take damage = stack count, then -1 stack |
| **Burn** | Take damage = stack count (doesn't go away!) |
| **Weakness** | -1 damage per stack (loses 1 per turn) |
| **Vulnerable** | Take 50% more damage (loses 1 per turn) |
| **Fatigued** | -1 stamina next turn, then goes away |
| **Hinder** | -1 damage per stack this turn only |
| **Exhausted** | Can't play cards! (loses 1 per turn) |
| **Scared** | Can't play ATTACK cards this turn |
| **Wet** | Makes you take bonus damage from some attacks |
| **Decay** | Reduces all healing by 5 per stack (PERMANENT!) |
